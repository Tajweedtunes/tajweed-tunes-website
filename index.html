About Tajweed Tunes

Tajweed Tunes is a Qur'anic and educational platform dedicated to promoting excellence in Qur'an recitation, Tajweed, memorization, revision and beneficial Islamic education. We are committed to nurturing learners with strong Qur'anic foundations, sound character and a lasting connection with the Qur'an.

Our Vision

To become a trusted platform for Qur'anic excellence, nurturing generations who recite, memorize, understand and live by the teachings of the Qur'an.

Our Mission

To provide quality Qur'anic education through effective Tajweed instruction, memorization, revision and beneficial Islamic learning in a supportive and structured environment.

Our Core Values

  • Qur'anic Excellence
  • Integrity
  • Discipline
  • Continuous Learning
  • Service & Impact

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to some of the most common questions about Tajweed Tunes services and programmes.

What services does Tajweed Tunes offer?

Tajweed Tunes offers Qur'an recitation, Tajweed classes, Qur'an memorization, Muraja'ah, online Qur'an classes and other Qur'anic and educational programmes.

Do you offer online Qur'an classes?

Yes. Tajweed Tunes provides online Qur'anic learning for students who require flexible and guided learning.

Can I join the Muraja'ah programme?

Yes. Students interested in strengthening their Qur'an retention through structured revision can enquire through our Registration & Enquiry form.

How can I register or make an enquiry?

Simply use the Registration & Enquiry section on this website. Fill in your details and send your enquiry directly through WhatsApp.

How can I contact Tajweed Tunes?

You can contact Tajweed Tunes through WhatsApp, phone call or email. Our contact details are available in the Contact section.

Who can join Tajweed Tunes programmes?

Our programmes are designed for learners interested in improving their Qur'an recitation, Tajweed, memorization and revision. Programme suitability can be discussed during enquiry.
